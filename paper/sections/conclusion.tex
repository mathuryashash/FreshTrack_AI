Near-perfect accuracy on public fresh/stale benchmarks says more about the
evaluation protocol than about the models. Removing augmented copies changes
little for a CNN and a great deal for a weak baseline. Holding out whole capture
sessions exposes a larger drop, concentrated where the photographs of fresh and
stale produce came from different sources, and neither per-image nor per-photo
splits can show it. On the photo-grouped split, one multi-task
MobileNetV3-Large recognises produce type and freshness as well as separate
EfficientNet-B0 models at lower cost, runs offline on a phone, and rejects most
images that are not produce. The next step is data rather than architecture.
Photographs from many sessions, places and devices, taken until the produce
spoils, are needed before shelf life can be predicted from RGB images at all.
